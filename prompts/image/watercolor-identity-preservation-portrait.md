# Watercolor Identity Preservation Portrait Prompt

## Purpose

Reusable image generation prompt for transforming a real person photo into a traditional watercolor and ink illustration.

Principles:
- Preserve identity first.
- Only transform visual style.
- Do not force gender, age, accessories, clothing, or scene changes.

## Prompt

Use the uploaded reference image as the only identity reference.

Create a watercolor portrait transformation of the same person.

Identity preservation is the highest priority:
Preserve the person's recognizable facial identity, including face shape, facial proportions, eyes, eyebrows, nose, lips, jawline, cheekbones, hairstyle, skin tone, and natural expression.

Do not change the person's gender presentation, age appearance, facial characteristics, or create a different person.

Do not add new accessories, clothing, hairstyle changes, makeup, jewelry, glasses, hats, straps, or decorative elements unless they already exist in the reference image.

Only transform the visual style.

---

## Style Transformation

Repaint the original image as a highly expressive traditional watercolor and ink illustration.

The watercolor effect should be strong and dominant:

- rich transparent watercolor layers
- visible wet-on-wet watercolor blending
- flowing pigment diffusion
- natural watercolor blooms
- soft color bleeding
- visible paper texture
- layered brush strokes
- organic watercolor edges
- hand-painted imperfections
- subtle pigment granulation
- spontaneous watercolor splashes and droplets

The image should feel like a real artist painted it by hand on watercolor paper.

Use delicate pen-and-ink linework:

- fine hand-drawn contours
- elegant sketch lines
- subtle ink variations
- loose artistic outlines
- expressive but natural details

Avoid a digital painting feeling.

---

## Portrait Style

Create a refined chest-up artistic portrait.

Keep the original person's natural pose and expression.

Enhance:

- soft watercolor shading on the face
- gentle light transitions
- realistic facial structure
- painterly skin texture
- expressive eyes
- natural hair strokes

The person should remain realistic and recognizable, but clearly become a watercolor artwork.

---

## Background

Remove the original background completely.

Create a clean minimal watercolor paper background:

- warm white watercolor paper texture
- subtle paint washes
- soft abstract watercolor clouds
- scattered pigment spots
- elegant empty space

The focus should remain entirely on the person.

---

## Color Style

Use a sophisticated watercolor palette:

- soft natural skin tones
- muted blue
- warm beige
- gray-green
- earthy brown
- gentle shadows

Avoid:

- oversaturated colors
- artificial digital colors
- glossy rendering
