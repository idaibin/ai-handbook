P01-KF02_LONG_LABOR_STATE acceptance checklist

HARD REQUIREMENTS
- exactly one independent image
- vertical 9:16 composition
- same person as both references
- same age; no ageing or face replacement
- same compact bun, clothing system, room, table and writing-slip system
- visibly more fatigued than KF01 without exaggerated suffering
- paused writing action and believable stiff-finger gesture
- coherent hands, brush and writing surface
- simple brush with no metal ferrule
- plain unpatterned clothing
- no obvious modern objects
- no readable text
- no report, dashboard, table, UI, title, label, logo or watermark

CONTINUITY HOLDS
- do not enlarge or ornament the bun
- do not broaden the body into a warrior build
- do not shift toward Edo, ronin, samurai, kimono or shoji aesthetics
- do not change room geometry or camera family
- do not change the textured hand-drawn historical illustration style

FAIL IF
- wrong person or clear age drift
- heroic, military or battlefield framing
- Japanese-period visual coding becomes dominant
- malformed hands or brush/surface discontinuity
- modern paper, books, furniture, glass or stationery appears
- any readable text or report-like layout appears
