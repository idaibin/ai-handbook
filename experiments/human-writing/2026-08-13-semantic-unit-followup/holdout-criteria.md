# Frozen holdout criteria

- H01: exactly one bullet; capability, freshness limit, and caller responsibility remain together; all facts and protected spans preserved.
- H02: exactly two bullets; pre-release backup is separate; post-release check stays with its threshold-triggered rollback and log retention.
- H03: exactly two bullets; public-report permission is separate; attachment prohibition stays with the de-identification-and-approval exception.
- H04: exactly three bullets, pairing each capability/evidence with its limitation; all six facts preserved.
- H05: exactly three numbered items on separate lines, in order, with no merge or invention.
- H06: exactly one paragraph, no bullets; preserve local/remote and runnable/production-ready boundaries.

A case passes only if its structural condition and all source facts, relations, modality, actors, protected spans, and explicit format constraints are preserved.
