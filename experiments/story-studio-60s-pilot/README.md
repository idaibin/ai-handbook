## 2. Idea, Audience, Genre & Hook Architecture

### 2.1 Core Premise (Idea)
In a dystopian high-density neon metropolis where human memory is commodified and traded, a memory salvage hacker discovers an encrypted neural fragment belonging to her supposedly deceased partner—revealing that the city's central memory vault is deleting citizens in real time.

### 2.2 Target Audience & Genre
- **Primary Audience:** 18–35 tech-savvy mobile viewers interested in Sci-Fi, suspense thrillers, cyberpunk aesthetics, and AI-native short dramas.
- **Genre Classification:** Sci-Fi / Suspense / Cyberpunk Micro-Drama.
- **Platform Format:** 9:16 Vertical Short-Form Video (TikTok, YouTube Shorts, Douyin, Bilibili Story).

### 2.3 60-Second Hook Architecture
```text
00:00 - 00:03  HOOK: Extreme close-up on biometric eye scanner flashing critical RED warning; audio glitch.
00:03 - 00:15  SETUP: Hacker Maya decrypts illegal neural drive in a rainy neon alley; establishes high stakes.
00:15 - 00:30  ESCALATION: Audio waveform matches her dead partner's voice; drone surveillance alarm triggers.
00:30 - 00:45  MIDPOINT TWIST: Partner's recorded voice says: "Maya, if you hear this, you are next."
00:45 - 00:55  CLIMAX: Enforcer drones breach the hideout window; energy weapons charge up.
00:55 - 01:00  CLIFFHANGER: Maya triggers EMP device; screen cuts to black with sound of breaking glass.
```

## 3. World Bible & Character Bible

### 3.1 World Bible
- **Setting:** Sector 7, Neo-Kowloon Metropolis (Year 2089).
- **Aesthetic Rules:**
  - Wet asphalt, perpetual light rain, dense holographic neon signs (#FF0055 Magenta, #00E5FF Cyan, #FFE600 Amber).
  - Heavy contrast between grimy brutalist alleyways and sleek towering corporate spires above the cloud layer.
  - Analog-cyberpunk hybrid: bulky wires, glowing optical chips, CRT monitor terminals alongside advanced holographic HUDs.

### 3.2 Character Bible
- **Protagonist: Maya Chen (陈麦)**
  - *Role:* Memory Salvage Tech / Ex-Corporate Archivist.
  - *Visual Consistency Tokens:* 26-year-old East Asian female, sharp jawline, asymmetrical short black hair with electric blue dyed fringe, luminous cyan cybernetic implant over the anatomical right temple (viewer-left in a front-facing render).
  - *Wardrobe Lock:* Weathered oversized matte-black waterproof techwear parka with high collar, orange inner lining, fingerless grip gloves.
  - *Signature Prop:* Custom brass-and-acrylic neural interface decoder with blinking amber LEDs.
  - *Palette:* Cyan (#00E5FF), Matte Black (#111111), Amber Accent (#FF9900).

- **Antagonist (Offscreen Voice / Threat): Vault Overseer & Hunter Drone Unit 9**
  - *Visual Consistency Tokens:* Sleek matte-white quad-rotor drone with central crimson scanning eye (#FF0033).
  - *Voice Profile:* Cold synthetic baritone with subtle algorithmic vocoder harmonics.

## 5. 60-Second Production Screenplay

```text
SCENE 1 - EXT. RAIN-SOAKED ALLEYWAY - NIGHT [00:00 - 00:15]
Rain beats against rusted ventilation grates.
MAYA (26) crouches behind a trash dumpster, fingers rapidly entering commands into her handheld decoder.
A crimson alert flashes on her cyan temple implant.

MAYA (VO / BREATHLESS)
(00:01) Thirty seconds before the grid locks me out...

The decoder screen beeps green. [ACCESS GRANTED - MEMORY FILE #8904].

SCENE 2 - INT. DILAPIDATED HACKER DEN - NIGHT [00:15 - 00:45]
Maya plugs the neural shard into the central terminal.
Holographic waveforms expand in mid-air, casting blue light across Maya's stunned face.

PARTNER'S VOICE (AUDIO RECORDING / GLITCHY)
(00:22) Maya... they lied about the purge. The vault isn't storing memories... it's harvesting minds.

Maya's eyes widen. A distant mechanical hum rattles the glass window.

PARTNER'S VOICE (CONT'D)
(00:32) If you're hearing this... you are already on the termination list.

SCENE 3 - INT. HACKER DEN - NIGHT [00:45 - 00:60]
RED SEARCHLIGHTS sweep through the rain-streaked window.
A Hunter Drone smashes through the glass, quad rotors screaming, crimson targeting laser painting Maya's chest.

MAYA
(00:50) Not today.

Maya slams her palm onto an EMP detonator on the desk.
The detonator glows blinding white.

CUT TO BLACK.
SFX: Blinding electrical discharge, shattered glass, high-pitched ringing.
TITLE CARD: CYBER-ECHOES: THE LAST SIGNAL — EPISODE 2 NEXT.
```

## 12. Feedback Schema & Cost/Quality Accounting Model

### 12.1 Pre-registered retention hypotheses
- **3-Second Retention Target:** ≥ 65%
- **30-Second Midpoint Retention:** ≥ 45%
- **60-Second Completion Rate:** ≥ 28%
- **Rewatch Rate Target:** ≥ 12%

### 12.2 Actual cost ledger required at execution

No provider, price or total cost is currently verified. Each attempt records provider,
effective model/tool version, billable unit, quantity, observed unit price/currency,
subtotal, retries, unusable outputs and observation time. The final ledger sums actual
observed attempts; it must not copy a marketing price or this plan's estimate.

### 12.3 Quality Scoring Rubric (1-5 Scale)
1. **Character Visual Consistency:** 1 = Face morphs between shots, 5 = Flawless identity lock.
2. **Motion Naturalism & Artifacts:** 1 = Severe rubber-banding/distortion, 5 = Cinematic physics.
3. **Audio-Visual Pacing & Sync:** 1 = Desynced voice/SFX, 5 = Exact frame-accurate impact.
4. **Narrative Retention Impact:** 1 = Boring/confusing, 5 = Compelling cliffhanger.
