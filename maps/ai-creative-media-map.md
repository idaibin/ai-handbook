# AI Creative & Media Map v0.1

## Purpose

AI Creative & Media defines the capability map for AI-assisted digital content creation, editing, management and distribution.

This map focuses on stable capabilities and workflows, not temporary products or model rankings.

## Scope

```text
AI Creative & Media

├── Image
├── Photography
├── Graphic Design
├── Social Content
├── Video
├── Animation
├── Comic
├── Digital Human
├── Audio
└── 3D / Spatial
```
